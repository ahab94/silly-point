## Silly Point v0.1.0: first release

A 3D cricket batting game about all the strange things a cricket ball does. **[Play in your browser](https://ahab94.github.io/silly-point/)**, or download for Windows, macOS or Linux below.

**What's in it**
- **Ten match situations**, each built around one quirk:
  - swing with the new ball, seam off a green top, bouncers;
  - Day 4 off-spin and the wrist-spinner's googly;
  - reverse swing with an old ball;
  - a night T20 chase in the dew;
  - saving a Test on a crumbling Day 5 pitch;
  - the second new ball.
- **The ball ages and the pitch wears.** Meters show the ball's shine, roughness and hardness. Reverse swing kicks in when an old ball turns rough. Cracks make balls shoot or spit, and footmarks grip.
- **Read the ball cam.** At release you see the seam and the shiny side, or the way a spinner's ball will turn.
- **Classic console-cricket controls.** A direction picks your footwork and the side; separate keys play defend, ground shot, loft and sweep; or leave it. Timing and shot selection decide everything.
- **LBW done properly**, with reviews:
  - "Pigeon-Eye" ball tracking, umpire's call, and the "Snick-o-Scope" for edges;
  - the fielding side reviews too.
- **Comedy sound.** A duck quacks if you're out for nought, a sad trombone plays for other wickets, and a brass band celebrates boundaries.
- **Touch controls** on phones and tablets.

All names are invented. Everything is drawn and synthesized in code.
