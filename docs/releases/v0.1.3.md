## Silly Point v0.1.3: made for phones

**[Play in your browser](https://ahab94.github.io/silly-point/)**, on your phone, tablet or computer.

- **Touch controls**, shown automatically on touch screens:
  - **Left thumb:** the stick picks direction and footwork. Up is front foot, down is back foot, left and right pick the leg or off side.
  - **Right thumb:** a big HIT button, with LOFT, BLOCK, SWEEP and CHARGE around it.
  - **Leave the ball:** tap nothing.
  - **Top corner:** MENU and CAM buttons.
- **Phones held upright** get their own layout:
  - the camera frames the batter for a tall screen;
  - text and buttons stay big enough to read and tap;
  - the commentary sits above the controls;
  - the menu shows touch help.
- **Phones held sideways** use the full widescreen layout, scaled to stay readable.
- **Lighter on phones:** no shadows and no anti-aliasing, so it runs smoothly.
