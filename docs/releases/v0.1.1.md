## Silly Point v0.1.1

**[Play in your browser](https://ahab94.github.io/silly-point/)**, or download below.

- **A new batting style.** The batter now:
  - stands upright and still, with the bat held just off the ground;
  - taps the bat while the bowler runs in;
  - makes a small back-and-across trigger movement before release;
  - picks the bat up high towards gully;
  - holds the high finish on the drives;
  - plays the flick with the wrists, the back leg kicking up behind.
- **A closer camera** behind the batter, so every stroke is easier to see.
- **Better phone controls:**
  - a big HIT button under the right thumb, with the other shots clustered around it;
  - the stick no longer covers the bowler panel.
- **No more invisible fielder.** When the bowler stops the ball in the follow-through, you now see the bowler do it. Fielders also dive when they misfield.
- **Snappier pacing** between balls. Review verdicts stay on screen long enough to read.
