## Silly Point v0.1.6: players with some meat on them

**[Play in your browser](https://ahab94.github.io/silly-point/)**

- **Real bodies, not sticks.** The limbs are shaped:
  - thighs with muscle, calves below the knee;
  - loose sleeves full at the shoulder and tapering to the wrist;
  - proper shoulders and hips.
- **Strokes use the whole body.** The shoulders coil in the backlift and unwind through the ball towards where it's going, with the hips leading. On drives the back heel lifts as the weight goes through. Back-foot shots pivot. The follow-through overshoots slightly and settles, like a real swing.
- **Everyone is alive.** Players breathe and shift their weight while standing, and outfielders walk in with the bowler's run-up.
- **Fixed:** in the defensive field, third man and fine leg were standing beyond the boundary rope.
