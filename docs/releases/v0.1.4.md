## Silly Point v0.1.4: easier timing, sound on phones

**[Play in your browser](https://ahab94.github.io/silly-point/)**

- **Easier timing.** The window to time each shot is about 40% wider, and "Perfect" is easier to hit. The timing bar's green zone shows the new size.
- **Sound works on iPhones in silent mode.** The browser version now plays as media after your first tap, the way video sites do, so the ring/silent switch doesn't mute the game.
- **Louder overall.** The overall volume is back up so phone speakers can be heard. The music and crowd stay in the background.
