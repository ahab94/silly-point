## Silly Point v0.1.5: polish pass

**[Play in your browser](https://ahab94.github.io/silly-point/)**

Bugs fixed after watching full matches, from the menu to the result card, on desktop and on phones:

- **The batter is back at the crease.** After a wicket, the next batter sometimes started the ball somewhere down the pitch, because the walk-off animation kept running into the next delivery.
- **No dead time.** The chase to the ball or the rope now runs at double speed after the shot, and the score comes up as the ball is thrown in.
- **No leftover scoreboard.** The next match's card no longer opens over the last match's score.
- **Readable on phones:**
  - the tip has a dark panel behind it;
  - the tip no longer covers the bowler panel;
  - the timing bar sits above the touch controls instead of on top of them;
  - the buttons say "Review", "Retry" and "Next" without keyboard hints.
- **Fielders running away from the camera** are lit properly instead of showing as black silhouettes.
- **A new self-check runs during every frame of the automated tests.** It catches stuck screens, impossible scores, the ball going through the ground, and players out of place. It reported no problems across 10 long sessions.
