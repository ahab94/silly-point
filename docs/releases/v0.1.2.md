## Silly Point v0.1.2: better players, calmer sound

**[Play in your browser](https://ahab94.github.io/silly-point/)**, or download below.

- **Much more detailed players:**
  - shaped bodies with shoulders, a belt and collar, and long sleeves;
  - faces, ears and hair;
  - proper shoes;
  - ribbed, strapped pads and padded gloves;
  - a helmet with a grille and neck guard;
  - a bat with a spine, grip and sticker.
- **A calmer look.** Softer sunlight, more natural grass, and off-white kit instead of glaring white.
- **New sound: sporty, not loud.**
  - A stadium anthem with a cheeky kazoo and a cowbell.
  - A soft air horn and drum for boundaries.
  - A quiet crowd that only reacts to the big moments.
  - Everything is turned down.
  - The comedy stays: the duck, the sad trombone, the slide whistle.
- The keeper stands a little wider to spinners, so the batting view is clear.
